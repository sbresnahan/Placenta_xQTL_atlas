# About this atlas {-}

The **Placenta xQTL Atlas** integrates multi-ancestry molecular QTL mapping with cross-ancestry fine-mapping, GWAS colocalization, transcriptome-wide association analysis, cell-type attribution, genotype × pre-pregnancy BMI interaction analysis, and locus-level synthesis.

The chapters present the analytical results and their biological interpretation as a single report. Each analysis layer is followed by focused literature context where applicable, and the final synthesis chapter prioritizes loci supported by multiple independent lines of evidence.

## Reading order {-}

The book is organized in four parts: regulatory architecture, GWAS integration, biological context, and integrated synthesis. The analytical report for each layer is followed by its literature context when a companion review is available.
