Of the 606 raw libraries, 605 were Salmon-quantified and QU-corrected and appear in the assembled modality BEDs (one metadata-only entry, SRR13696952, was never quantified). Ancestry assignment and the genotype intersection with PCA-outlier removal leave 285 EAS and 156 EUR RNA-seq samples; collapsing technical replicates to one column per individual gives the final mapping sets of 281 EAS and 136 EUR individuals.

Two stage-specific notes. (1) Normalization (INT) runs upstream of the genotype intersection on one run per individual, so INT-stage membership (282 EAS / 141 EUR for the non-expression modalities; the expression INT BEDs were no longer present at archive time and are carried forward from the genotype-intersection stage) counts a single run per individual, whereas QTL-input membership (285 EAS / 156 EUR) flags every QC-passing run whose individual survives to mapping — both runs of a technical-replicate pair map to the same individual (array_id) column. Run-level counts can therefore exceed INT-stage counts without any sample gain; phenotype values for replicated individuals come from the single normalized run, run-level covariates are averaged across the pair, and the operative mapping sets are the per-individual counts. (2) Splicing is the only modality with QTL-input attrition (284 EAS / 155 EUR), from junction-level harmonization.

PC-AiR coordinates are not in this archive (run 35_extract_report_extras.py –only pcair); the within-stratum view below is shown instead.

Within-stratum PC1–PC2 scatter for the final mapping sets (281 EAS, 136 EUR individuals, post 6-SD outlier removal). No residual substructure clusters are visible at this scale in either stratum.

Scree plots (cohort-only PCA, LD-pruned variants): PC1 carries ≈ 13–14% of pruned-variant variance in both strata, with a smooth, shallow decay thereafter (PC2 ≈ 6–9%) and no sharp elbow — the expected spectrum within ancestry-stratified subsets, and the basis for the first-5-PCs rule.

Genotype PCs colored by cohort. On both the shared PC-AiR axes (left) and the pooled within-stratum axes (right), cohorts of the same assigned ancestry occupy distinct but adjacent regions — GUSTO and SNUH resolve within EAS, NIEHS_RICHS and NIGMS within EUR — reflecting fine-scale ancestry composition and platform differences between cohorts. This structure is absorbed by the within-stratum genotype PCs (PC1-5) carried as covariates in every QTL model; the spread of NIEHS_RICHS along pooled EUR PC1 is continuous within-cohort ancestry variation, not a discrete batch.

Per-cohort value distributions before (left column) and after (right column) normalization. For the ratio-based modalities (QN + within-cohort INT), cohort-specific location/scale shifts visible in the pooled unnormalized stage collapse onto a common standard-normal-like distribution after processing; quantile normalization aligns the distributions, and the per-cohort inverse-normal transform removes residual cohort location/scale differences by construction. For the two expression modalities (terminal TMM -> VST), cohort scale differences are NOT removed by construction — cohort enters through indicator covariates in the mapping model and in hidden-factor estimation, and cohort variance heterogeneity is re-verified by the diagnostic gate (29b-29f). After-stage labels marked ‘(lumped)’ pool cohorts whose array ids carry no cohort namespace — they were lumped at extraction time and cannot be split from the aggregated values (composition shown in the axis label); the PCA panels below resolve the same samples per cohort exactly. Re-running extraction with –geno-ancestry-map labels them per cohort upstream. Expression has no pooled-unnormalized stage (pooled unnormalized BEDs are saved only for the non-expression modalities), so its panel shows the final stage only.

Sample PCA per modality (rows: processing stage; columns: ancestry stratum), colored by cohort. For the ratio-based modalities (QN + within-cohort INT), cohort clustering in the pooled-unnormalized row that disappears in the normalized row indicates successful cohort-scale harmonization; residual clustering would motivate revisiting the cohort covariates in the mapping model. For the two expression modalities (terminal TMM -> VST), cohort structure may persist in the normalized row by design — it is absorbed by cohort indicator covariates rather than by the phenotype transform.
