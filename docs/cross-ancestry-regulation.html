5 Cross-ancestry regulation

Following DevBrain Fig. 2: per-ancestry discovery (2B analog) and cross-ancestry effect-size concordance (2C analog). When the enriched archive is present (extraction script 35 + aggregated SuSHiE outputs), this section additionally renders the lead-slope replication scatter (2C), showcase-locus tracks (2E), and credible-set summaries (2D analog); without it those panels skip with a note and the gene-level hexbin is the fallback concordance view. Cross-ancestry analysis here is comparative only (no pooled p-value combination).

5.1 Per-ancestry discovery comparison

Table 5.1: Combined-layer eGene overlap between ancestries (genes tested in both: 12,595).
set n
EAS only 1196
shared 2041
EUR only 3846

Of the 3237 EAS and 5887 EUR combined-layer eGenes, 2041 are shared (Fisher exact vs the tested-in-both universe: odds ratio 2.45, p = 1.5e-103) — shared eGenes are more frequent than expected under independence. The larger EUR discovery counts track its larger phenotype universe (ancestry-specific filtering; Landscape) rather than a larger mapping sample — EAS is the bigger stratum.

5.2 Ancestry-specific versus shared eGenes

DevBrain fig. S4 analog (adapted): composition of the EAS-only, shared, and EUR-only combined-layer eGene sets — gene biotype (panel a) and, when the constraint table is present, gnomAD LOEUF (panel b).

Shared eGenes are less constrained than ancestry-specific ones (median LOEUF 0.51 shared vs 0.43 specific; Wilcoxon p = 2.3e-10) — genes with cross-ancestry regulatory signal trend toward dosage sensitivity.

5.3 Cross-ancestry effect-size concordance

DevBrain Fig. 2C analog: consistency of xQTL effects between the two ancestry strata. When the cross-ancestry lookup table is present this is shown as the DevBrain discovery-vs-replication lead-slope scatter; otherwise the gene-level −log10(p) hexbin below is the fallback view.

Each point is an FDR ≤ 5% discovery-layer lead (variant, phenotype) pair; the y-axis is the same pair’s slope in the other ancestry from a targeted lookup (bars: ±1.96·SE). Slopes are on inverse-normal-transformed phenotypes — both strata share the transform, so slopes (not aFC) are the comparable effect scale here, a deliberate deviation from DevBrain’s aFC panel. Of 3,449 looked-up pairs, 1,127 (32.7%) replicate at nominal p < 0.05 and direction agrees in 74.7%.

5.4 Showcase loci

DevBrain Fig. 2E analog: regional association tracks for two showcase loci — ERAP2 (the literature-recurrent placenta eQTL) and the top shared non-expression-driver locus — per ancestry, points colored by LD (r²) to the lead variant (diamond), gene models below. Rendered only when the per-locus regional scans (locus_.tsv.gz, extraction script 35) are in the archive.*

The lead variant (purple diamond) sits in a TSS-proximal LD block; ancestry differences in the tracks reflect both LD structure and true effect heterogeneity, which the joint SuSHiE fine-mapping quantifies formally.
